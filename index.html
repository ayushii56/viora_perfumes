VIORA represents the poetry of proximity. Founded under the philosophy that memory is inextricably tied to smell, our master perfumers distill feelings of attraction, warm distance, and unforgettable presence into glass bottles.

We craft niche fragrances using absolute oils harvested responsibly, aged with patience, and hand-poured in premium, heavy-weight glass silhouettes. We design scents not for the room, but for the skin.
